## Failures found and repairs

1. This machine required isolated Playwright/Chromium and bytecode-cache paths plus its local Odoo,
   addon-path and action configuration. Those were environment adaptations only.
2. The browser runner initially armed response waits too late and used a page-global Spanish send
   label that also matched Odoo chatter. The harness now arms waits with the triggering click and
   scopes the send control to the Assistant composer; assertions were not weakened.
3. The real gate then exposed a product defect: an approved effectful turn persisted the approval
   proposal as one Assistant message and created a second final message after verification. Turn
   completion now reuses the already-linked Assistant message, and the frontend reconciles the
   provisional/final local projections after a decision. Focused Odoo and HOOT regressions cover the
   repaired transition.
