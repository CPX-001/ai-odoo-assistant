## Accepted behavior

- Model, reasoning and autonomy preferences render as one compact row of 28 px pills.
- Closed pills use bounded symbols/tokens; open menus preserve the full localized labels and explanatory text.
- The reasoning menu exposes `none`, `minimal`, `low`, `medium` and `high`, ending at **Alto**.
- Provider-only `xhigh`, `max` and `ultra` values are not exposed by this product surface.
- The model menu contains no selection tick and adds no custom submenu arrow; Odoo supplies the single native dropdown/submenu affordance.
- Model selection remains visible through a subtle selected-row background.
- The send action remains aligned to the right edge of the composer.
